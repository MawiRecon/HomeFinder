Bookmarklet setup

A one-click way to send a Zillow listing to your HomeFinder dashboard.

1. Your dashboard URL

Paste the URL where this dashboard is hosted (e.g. https://yourname.github.io/HomeFinder/). We'll use it once now to generate your bookmarklet — it isn't stored anywhere.

2. Drag this to your bookmarks bar

+ Add to HomeFinder

Show your bookmarks bar with Ctrl+Shift+B if it's hidden.

3. Use it

  1. Browse to any Zillow listing page.
  2. Click the + Add to HomeFinder bookmark.
  3. A new tab opens with the dashboard, modal pre-filled — review, save.

What it captures

It tries, in order: JSON-LD schema data, Zillow's internal page data, then meta tags. Most listings give us: URL, address, city/state/zip, lat/lng, price, beds, baths, sqft, photo, home type. Anything missing you'll see blank in the form and can fill in manually.

Privacy

The bookmarklet runs entirely in your browser — no third-party server. Data goes from the Zillow tab straight to your dashboard tab via a URL parameter.
